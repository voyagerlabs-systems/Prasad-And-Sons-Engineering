About Us

Industrial Capability. Practical Execution.

Prasad & Sons Engineering is a steel structure erection and PUF panel installation contractor. Our main focus is complete industrial shed and warehouse contracts, delivered as one coordinated scope from site marking through to final flashing and finishing.

Led by Avinash Prasad, whose industry experience spans more than two decades, and supported by on-site supervision, the business combines hands-on knowledge with organised coordination — so every engagement starts with a clearly defined scope and ends with work measured and closed against it. We also take on individual installation contracts and labour subcontracting assignments.

Services

One coordinated contract for your complete shed.

Our primary service is the end-to-end steel structure and PUF panel shed contract. Each stage is also available as an individual contract.

Additional Services

Individual contracts and labour subcontracting.

For builders, fabricators, PEB companies and industrial contractors who need a specific stage executed.

Site marking, foundation and flooring work are coordinated services carried out to the client's approved drawings and design. Structural design, engineering approvals and certification remain with the client's appointed engineer and are not provided by us. Drawings shown are illustrative.
